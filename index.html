A day that feels manageable

Build a rhythm that works.

DayFlow keeps the small steps visible, so you can spend less time organising and more time actually doing.

01 · PLAN

Choose your priorities

Start with the few tasks that matter most. Give them a due date and a priority, then keep the rest of the list in perspective.

02 · FOCUS

Give work its space

Use focused sessions to make progress without constantly switching context. Your completed sessions feed into your weekly review.

03 · REFLECT

Notice the progress

Check off habits, review completed tasks and see how your focused time adds up over the week.

04 · RESET

Make space to pause

Leave room between tasks. A realistic plan gives you breathing space when something takes longer than expected.

05 · BUILD

Repeat the habits

Use small daily check-ins to turn intentions into routines you can actually maintain.

06 · REVIEW

Start fresh tomorrow

Look back at what moved forward, carry over what matters and let go of the rest.

Morning · Set direction

Pick your top priorities, check the calendar and decide what a good day would look like.

Afternoon · Protect focus

Work through one task at a time, record focus sessions and take proper breaks.

Evening · Close the loop

Check off habits, capture loose thoughts and prepare a simple starting point for tomorrow.

Weekly · See the bigger picture

Review completed tasks, focused time and consistency across the week.

A flexible routine, not another system to keep up with. Change your plan whenever the day changes.

A clearer, more dependable glass layer.

Version 1.5.0 rebuilds the Windows glass host so the desktop backdrop can appear behind DayFlow without fading text or controls. The transparency slider now adjusts a wider acrylic tint range, and the release history has a dedicated in-app page. Your tasks, notes, habits, goals and settings remain intact.
